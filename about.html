About Nairobi Pulse

Nairobi Pulse is a Kenyan digital media platform dedicated to delivering honest, timely, and relevant content to readers across Kenya and the East African region.

Our Mission

We believe every Kenyan deserves access to quality information — whether it's breaking news, lifestyle inspiration, tech insights, or health guidance. Our mission is to inform, inspire, and connect the Kenyan community through compelling storytelling.

What We Cover

Our editorial team covers a wide range of topics including national and regional news, lifestyle and culture, technology and innovation, health and wellness, entertainment, and personal finance.

Our Team

Nairobi Pulse is run by a passionate team of writers, editors, and digital creators based in Nairobi, Kenya. We are committed to accuracy, fairness, and journalistic integrity in everything we publish.
